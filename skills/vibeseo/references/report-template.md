# Reusable deliverables

## Report header

Site and business goal; market/language/device; data dates and coverage; evidence mode (live, supplied, or synthetic); selected operations; approved scope; collection status; charge receipt status; unknowns that affect the decision.

Use actual source identifiers and URLs. Prefer short readable evidence IDs linked to the local ledger over repeating raw run metadata everywhere. Reports must exclude credentials and unnecessary personal data.

## Keyword/page map

| Cluster / keyword | Intent and evidence | Demand, source/date | Organic difficulty if supplied | Existing verified URL or proposed page | Business fit / original contribution | Priority and reason | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Unknown fields stay Unknown with their reason. Ad competition and organic difficulty are separate. A content calendar contains proposed publish dates, page owners, asset needs and review status; it is not a scheduled publisher.

## Content brief

- Target keyword, audience, searcher's job, conversion goal and observed/supplied SERP context.
- Existing page overlap and proposed URL/page type.
- Two or three title options, accurate meta summary and H1.
- H2/H3 outline: each section's question, contribution, evidence and example.
- Common coverage versus the site's original contribution; first-hand evidence/assets required.
- Real sources to cite and factual claims still needing verification.
- Verified internal-link URLs/anchors, or clearly proposed candidates awaiting page inventory.
- CTA, author/editor review, accessibility and applicable visible-content-matching schema checks.
- Optional requested draft, with unresolved facts flagged and publication pending.

States: Discovered, Needs verification, Qualified, Drafted, Contacted, Responded, Placement observed. Every action beyond Drafted needs separate authorization and actual evidence. Heuristic relevance, DR/DA and real placements are distinct fields; do not invent missing metrics.

## Finish every artifact

Give the next 3–5 actions with owner, effort, evidence and verification; identify missing information; record exactly what was prepared or observed. Save only in the visible chosen local folder. No automatic outreach, content publication, recurring collection or external sharing is created.
